# Marketplace workflow completion

Keep the established design. Extend the existing customer/staff/professional system rather than creating another checkout.

1. Bespoke quotes: customers describe work for a published service and covered address. Owner proposes a bounded, itemized rupee quote and duration; customer accepts the exact revision. Checkout snapshots that quote into an ordinary booking with existing capacity reservation, payment, cancellation, refunds and partner ledger. No amendments to paid totals. Staff may view requests, only Owner may price them. Expiry, ownership, duplicate booking and revision races must be enforced server-side.
2. Home Care Passport: private assets, saved services, completed booking history grouped by address, and recurring care reminders. Reminders do not charge or automatically book. Account deletion and backups cover all new data.
3. Laundry fulfillment: configurable service workflow, sequential collection/processing/delivery evidence, authorized partner/staff updates, and completion only after delivery. Do not publish invented laundry prices.

Business dependencies remain final prices, coverage, staffing, terms and warranties. Actual subscription auto-debits and prepaid credits require approved refund/expiry rules and are excluded from reminder functionality.

Reference journeys: Urban Company service discovery, booking management/rebooking and support (https://www.urbancompany.com/near-me/); NoBroker service catalog (https://www.nobroker.in/home-services); HomeTriangle project requests (https://hometriangle.com/). Use these as functional references, not copied branding.
