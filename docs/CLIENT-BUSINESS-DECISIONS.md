# Business decisions for tonight's client review

The catalog and client demo are ready for review. These decisions determine the actual launch configuration; sample content is not an approved business policy.

| Decision | What the client needs to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Services and pricing | Which services launch first, package prices, paid extras, diagnosis/quotation rules, consumables, exclusions and repair approval before additional work |
| Service area | PIN codes, operating hours, visit duration, travel buffers, same-day cut-off and holidays |
| Staff and capacity | Real approved professionals, employee/partner model, skills, availability, verification responsibility and substitute/no-show procedure |
| Customer payments | Advance/full payment, supported methods, tax handling, receipt/invoice requirements and merchant of record |
| Partner earnings | Commission, settlement timing, withdrawal approval, fees, disputes and reconciliation owner |
| Cancellations/refunds | Cut-off, professional/customer cancellation, refunds, revisits, damage reporting, written guarantees and escalation ownership |
| Support | Staffed phone/email, operating hours, response targets, emergency procedure and person accountable for unresolved complaints |
| Legal | Approved terms/privacy, data retention, account deletion, professional agreement, required consents and policy version |
| Accounts and hosting | Client Vercel team/domain, API host with persistent disk, Firebase/EAS ownership, billing responsibility, GitHub access and handover |
| Launch evidence | Successful test checkout/refund/webhook, live payment certification, security review, off-site restore drill, real-device sign-in/push and release approval |

Enter agreed service details in Owner → Services, company policy/coverage in Owner → Settings, and onboard real professionals. Financial/legal terms that require new behavior (for example partial refunds or GST invoices) must be designed and verified before enabling them. Existing refunds are full refunds.
