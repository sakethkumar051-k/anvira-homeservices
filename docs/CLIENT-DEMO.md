# ANVIRA client demo — five minutes

Open **http://localhost:4001/demo**. The page signs into each fictional role when you select a chapter. You can also open **http://localhost:4001/?sample=customer** directly. The Firebase app on port 4000 is separate.

All people, service prices, reviews, offers and bookings in this demo are fictional. Payment and refund buttons simulate the workflow. No client payment is collected.

1. **Customer, two minutes.** Open Doorstep car cleaning. Choose Inside & out, vehicle size and water access. Enter the car model and parking instructions. The app explains what is included and excluded, asks only relevant questions, and shows the selected price and duration. Add a saved address, pick an available slot and try `DEMO100`. Continue to payment and simulate it. Reopening checkout restores a draft; a draft does not reserve a slot.
2. **Professional, one minute.** Open Ravi's jobs. An accepted sample car clean is ready. Inspect the selected package, vehicle, parking instructions and customer conversation. Record arrival, start the job, complete all service-specific and standard tasks, then leave useful handover notes. Open the professional profile to demonstrate bio, languages, photo editing and availability. Select See partner earnings in the guide to inspect Suresh’s completed kitchen job, commission and sample wallet balance.
3. **Owner, one minute.** Inspect the overview and launch readiness panel. The seeded completed sofa service demonstrates service value, history, review and reporting. Open Services to edit packages, priced choices, required customer questions, preparation, scope, exclusions, FAQs and completion tasks. Inspect the cancelled AC booking's full-refund request; only the Owner approves it.
4. **Customer Care, one minute.** Open the sample sofa support conversation. Reply with specific guidance, explain the resolution and close the request. A customer follow-up reopens it. Quality concerns have a one-hour initial response target; general requests have a four-hour target. The worker escalates overdue requests once per waiting period.

The catalog contains: home deep cleaning, doorstep car cleaning, AC service/repair, plumbing, electrical diagnosis, painting consultation, bathroom cleaning, sofa cleaning, washing-machine diagnosis and furniture assembly. Every entry includes tailored scope, exclusions, preparation, FAQs and professional tasks. Diagnosis and consultation prices explicitly exclude later repairs or painting work.

Two saved addresses, the `DEMO100` offer, car booking, completed sofa and partner kitchen bookings, sample earnings and support messages and AC refund request populate the review database. The versioned seed runs once and preserves subsequent edits and demo history. Run `npm run seed:demo` to apply missing demo migrations, then `npm run demo` to build and serve it. There is no automatic reset. Ten sample service drafts are also available in the Firebase Owner app on port 4000, kept inactive until you review and approve their real scope and prices. Fictional customers, professionals, bookings and reviews stay in the local demo.
